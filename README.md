# BlockForge

A single-player voxel first-person shooter for the browser. Blocky world, cinematic lighting, two game modes, and a full progression and ranked system. Every other player in the game is an AI competitor.

Everything is original and generated by the game's code: textures, voxel models, sounds and music. There are no image, audio or model asset files.

## Running it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:5173
npm run build      # type-check + production bundle in dist/
npm run preview    # serve dist/ on http://localhost:4173
npm run test:e2e   # headless smoke test of boot, all modes and persistence
```

You need a browser with WebGL 2: current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, with hardware acceleration turned on.

## Modes

**Battle Royale (Ashcrown Isle).** 24 competitors ride an airship over a large handcrafted island and drop in. The island has meadows, forest, a snow-capped mountain range, desert mesas, a river delta, villages, a castle, a mine with tunnels, a lighthouse, a dam and other landmarks. Players loot weapons, ammo, armor, heals, grenades and materials from the ground and from supply chests. They harvest wood, stone and metal and use them to build walls, floors, ramps and blocks, and they craft at any time. A storm closes in over six phases, and the last player alive wins.

**Team Deathmatch.** 6v6 on three purpose-built arenas: *Hamlet* (sunset village), *Quarry* (noon, a terraced pit with a crane) and *Frostbite* (a snowy night outpost). You choose a loadout and respawn after each death. Spawn protection and restricted enemy spawn zones stop spawn camping. The first team to 50 kills wins, or the team ahead when the 8-minute clock runs out. Building is disabled in this mode.

**Training Grounds.** An interactive tutorial with 14 steps: movement, looting, shooting, reloading, aiming, chests, sniper scopes, harvesting, building, crafting, grenades and a final duel.

Both competitive modes have **Casual** and **Ranked** playlists.

## Features

- **Rendering:** a custom HDR pipeline built on three.js. It has greedy-meshed voxel chunks with per-vertex ambient occlusion and smooth sky and block light, two-cascade soft shadow maps, screen-space reflections on water (with depth tint, ripples and shoreline foam), volumetric god rays, bloom, ACES tonemapping with colour grading, and FXAA. The sky, clouds, stars and moon are procedural, and cloud shadows move across the ground. Day/night lighting changes with the sun's height. Weather includes rain, storms with lightning and snow. Foliage moves in the wind.
- **Weapons:** 8 weapons built from voxels: pistol, SMG, two assault rifles, pump shotgun, DMR, bolt-action sniper and rocket launcher. Each has its own recoil pattern, spread, damage falloff and reload animation (empty and tactical reloads). Every gun has muzzle flash, ejected shells and impact effects. Bullets are hitscan, while sniper rounds and rockets are physical projectiles. Scopes have variable zoom and their own sensitivity. Frag and smoke grenades follow an arc that you preview before throwing.
- **AI competitors:** bots perceive, navigate (A* over the voxel grid), loot, rotate with the storm, take cover, heal, build, throw grenades and push or hold lanes. Each has an archetype, a skill level and a reaction time. Their aim errors are human-like rather than perfect.
- **Building and crafting:** grid-snapped pieces in wood, stone or metal with placement previews and collision checks. Each block has HP, and structures can be damaged and collapse. The world cannot be dug or tunnelled, which stops players sealing themselves in. Recipes cover ammo, heals, armor plates, grenades and material conversion.
- **Progression:** XP and levels with reward reveals, coins, daily login rewards, three daily quests, achievements, and earnable cosmetics (weapon camos, outfits, banners, titles). Cosmetics never affect gameplay, and nothing can be bought with real money.
- **Ranked:** 8 tiers (Flint → Forgemaster). Every tier below Forgemaster has 4 divisions (IV → I). You gain and lose RP each match, get promoted or demoted with demotion protection after a promotion, and play 8-week seasons with soft resets and rewards. The ranked ladder is made of simulated AI profiles.
- **Events:** limited-time weekend events run on the real calendar of your device. Countdowns show real time remaining and you can preview upcoming events.

## About the AI competitors

BlockForge is designed to feel like an online shooter, but there are no real players, servers, accounts or chat. Everyone in your lobby, on your team, in the kill feed and on the ladder is an AI player. Each one has a persistent name, look, play style, rank and match history, and they keep that identity across matches. The game labels them as AI in the lobby, the pause menu, the results screen and the ladder. The ladder compares you only with these simulated profiles. It is not a global ranking.

## Saving

Your profile, settings and the AI competitors' records are saved in `localStorage` on your device. Daily rewards, quests and events follow your device clock. You can erase all of it under **Settings → About → Reset progress**.

## Testing

`npm run test:e2e` drives the production bundle, so run `npm run build` first. The test starts its own preview server and uses headless Chromium. It boots the game, plays fast-forwarded slices of Battle Royale, Team Deathmatch and the tutorial, finishes each match through the results screen, and checks that progress survives a reload.

`tests/scenarios/` holds longer scripted sessions that also save screenshots to `tests/screens/`. Run them with `node tests/drive.mjs tests/scenarios/<name>.mjs` against `npm run preview`:

- `brfull`: a full ranked Battle Royale, logging every elimination.
- `tdm`: a full Team Deathmatch. Set `TDM_MAP` to choose the arena.
- `tutorial`: plays all 14 training steps through the real input layer.
- `ranked`: promotion, demotion, the ladder and a season rollover.
- `menutabs`: every menu tab, settings, and the daily reward and its claim limit.
- Diagnostics: `vm` (weapon viewmodels at the hip and aiming down sights), `build` (placement onto the training ledge), `brearly` (landing spread, bot accuracy and early eliminations), `perf` (simulation cost and a CPU profile) and `mem` (geometry and memory across modes).

  core/    input, settings, RNG, noise, maths, storage
  world/   blocks, voxel world, lighting, greedy mesher, maps/ (BR island, TDM arenas, training)

tests/     Playwright drivers and scenarios (`npm run test:e2e`)
