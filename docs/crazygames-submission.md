## Description

BlockForge is a blocky battle royale shooter. Jump out of the airship, glide down onto a voxel island, loot chests for guns, armor and grenades, throw up walls and ramps in a second and be the last one standing when the storm closes in.

Battle Royale: 24 players drop onto an island with villages, forests and a shrinking storm. Harvest wood, stone and metal, craft gear and build forts to win fights.

Team Deathmatch: fast 6 vs 6 rounds on three maps, Hamlet, Quarry and Frostbite. First team to 50 eliminations wins.

Weapons: SMG, two assault rifles, pump shotgun, DMR, bolt-action sniper with a real scope, rocket launcher, pistol, frag and smoke grenades.

Ranked: climb from Flint to Forgemaster. Opponents start easy and get tougher every rank, and each tier unlocks its own weapon camo.

Progression: daily rewards, quests, achievements, levels, a Lucky Wheel and a locker full of outfits, camos and banners.

New here? A one-minute hands-on tutorial gets you moving, shooting, looting and building, and the Training Grounds teach the rest.

All opponents are computer-controlled, so a match starts instantly at any time of day.

Plays on desktop and on phones and tablets with full touch controls. Graphics quality adjusts to your device.

## Controls

WASD: move
Mouse: look around
Left click: shoot / place a build piece
Right click: aim down sights / scope
Space: jump (in the air: open the glider)
C: crouch
Shift: sprint
R: reload (in build mode: rotate piece)
E: open chests, pick up loot
Q: build mode on/off
1-3: weapons, 4: heals, 5: throwables (in build mode 1-4: wall, floor, ramp, block)
Mouse wheel: scope zoom
X: switch build material (wood, stone, metal)
F: pickaxe (harvest materials)
G: throw grenade
H: heal
Tab: inventory and crafting (scoreboard in Team Deathmatch)
M: map
P (or Esc): pause and settings

Mobile: left thumb stick to move, drag the right side of the screen to look, on-screen buttons for shoot, aim, jump, crouch, reload, build, heal, grenade and loot.

All keys can be rebound in Settings.

## Ad scenarios

Midgame (153 characters):

Midgame ads only at natural breaks: before a match, between match end and results, and when leaving results. Never during play. Game is paused and muted.

Rewarded (249 characters):

Opt-in, reward after the ad: 2x XP+coins after a match, BR Redeploy (1x/match), BR starter kit, Rank Shield, Lucky Wheel spin, 200 coins (5 min cooldown), 2x XP 15 min, double daily reward, quest reroll, half-price shop item, Ad Vault skins (3 ads).

Banner (149 characters):

One bottom banner in menus, on results and while spectating after a BR death. Never during play, never covers UI. Refreshes every ~30s while visible.
